# Job Description Templates

Structured templates for writing job descriptions that attract the right candidates and set clear expectations.

---

**Reports to:** [Manager title]

**The role**

[2-3 sentences about why this role exists and what impact it has. What problem does this person solve? What does success look like in 6 months?]

**What you'll do**

- [Primary responsibility — the thing they'll spend most of their time on]
- [Second responsibility]
- [Third responsibility]
- [Fourth responsibility]
- [Fifth responsibility — keep it to 5-7 bullets max]

**What we're looking for**

Must-haves:
- [Required skill or experience — be specific: "3+ years managing paid ad campaigns" not "marketing experience"]
- [Required skill]
- [Required skill]

Nice-to-haves:
- [Preferred but not required]
- [Preferred but not required]

**What we offer**

- [Compensation detail]
- [Benefits — health, PTO, retirement]

- [Growth — training budget, career path, mentorship]

**How to apply**

[Instructions — what to send, where to send it, and what to include. "Send your resume and a short note about why this role interests you to hiring@company.com" is better than a generic portal link.]

## Template 2: Operations / Admin Role

---

### [Operations Manager / Office Manager / Executive Assistant]

**Location:** [Location]
**Type:** [Full-time / Part-time]
**Reports to:** [Owner / CEO / COO]
**Compensation:** [$XX,XXX - $XX,XXX]

---

**About us**

[Company description]

**The role**

You'll be the person who makes sure nothing falls through the cracks. This role owns the day-to-day systems that keep the business running — from scheduling and vendor management to process improvement and team coordination. If you're the kind of person who sees a broken process and can't help but fix it, this is your role.

**What you'll do**

- Manage daily operations and ensure workflows run smoothly across the team
- Own vendor relationships, contracts, and renewals
- Coordinate schedules, meetings, and travel
- Maintain and improve internal SOPs and documentation
- Track budgets, invoices, and expense reports
- Onboard new team members (systems access, equipment, orientation)
- Serve as the go-to person for "how do we do this?" questions

**What we're looking for**

Must-haves:
- [X]+ years of operations, office management, or administrative experience
- Proficiency with [your tools — Google Workspace, Notion, QuickBooks, etc.]
- Strong organizational skills — you manage multiple priorities without dropping balls
- Clear written and verbal communication
- Comfort with ambiguity — you'll build systems, not just follow them

Nice-to-haves:
- Experience in [your industry]
- Familiarity with project management tools (Asana, Monday, Notion)
- Bookkeeping or basic accounting knowledge

**What we offer**

- [Compensation and benefits]

## Tips

- **Lead with what the person will do, not who you are.** Candidates skim — put the role details above the company bio if your brand isn't well-known.
- **Be honest about compensation.** Listings without pay ranges get fewer qualified applicants. If you can't post the exact number, give a range.
- **Cut the jargon.** "Synergy," "rockstar," "fast-paced environment" — these are red flags to experienced candidates. Say what you mean.
- **Must-haves should be actual must-haves.** If you'd interview someone without it, it's a nice-to-have. Overstuffing requirements filters out strong candidates.
- **Describe the work, not the person.** "You'll manage a $50K ad budget across three platforms" is more useful than "You're a self-starter with a passion for digital marketing."
