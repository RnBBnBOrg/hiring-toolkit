Tips

  • Fill this out within 30 minutes of the interview. Your memory degrades fast. The notes you take now are the ones you'll rely on when comparing candidates later.
  • Score against the role requirements, not against other candidates. A "3" means they meet the bar, regardless of how other candidates did.
  • Write evidence, not impressions. "Described managing a $200K budget with zero overruns over 18 months" is better than "Seemed financially savvy."
  • Every interviewer fills out their own scorecard independently. Don't discuss scores before everyone has submitted — it prevents groupthink.
  • "Lean yes" is a no. If you're not confident, the answer is probably no. Hiring someone you're unsure about rarely works out.
