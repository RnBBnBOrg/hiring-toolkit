Tips

  • Lead with what the person will do, not who you are. Candidates skim — put the role details above the company bio if your brand isn't well-known.
  • Be honest about compensation. Listings without pay ranges get fewer qualified applicants. If you can't post the exact number, give a range.
  • Cut the jargon. "Synergy," "rockstar," "fast-paced environment" — these are red flags to experienced candidates. Say what you mean.
  • Must-haves should be actual must-haves. If you'd interview someone without it, it's a nice-to-have. Overstuffing requirements filters out strong candidates.
  • Describe the work, not the person. "You'll manage a $50K ad budget across three platforms" is more useful than "You're a self-starter with a passion for digital marketing."
