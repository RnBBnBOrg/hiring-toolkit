## Tips

- **Don't average away the signal.** A candidate with a 5 in the critical skill and a 2 in a nice-to-have is often better than a candidate with all 3s. Weight the competencies that matter most for this role.
- **Watch for "halo effect."** One great interview moment shouldn't override three mediocre scorecards. Look at the full picture.
- **Compensation is a factor, not the factor.** The cheapest candidate isn't always the best hire. Factor in ramp time, retention risk, and what they'll produce in Year 1.
- **If nobody clears the bar, don't hire.** An empty seat is painful. A bad hire is worse. If you're debating between two "lean no" candidates, reopen the search.
- **Document your reasoning.** When someone asks "why did we hire X?" six months from now, you want a clear answer. This matrix is that answer.
