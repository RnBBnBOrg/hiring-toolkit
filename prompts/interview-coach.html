AI Prompts: Interview Coach

Prompts to help you prepare for interviews, evaluate candidates, and improve your process.

Prompt 1: Generate Custom Interview Questions for a Specific Role

I'm interviewing candidates for a [Job Title] at my company. Here's what the role involves:

[Paste the job description or summarize the key responsibilities]

Generate 12 behavioral interview questions tailored to this specific role. For each question:

  • State what competency it's testing
  • Give me a "strong answer" example (what a great candidate would say)
  • Give me a "red flag" example (what should concern me)

Focus on questions that reveal how they actually work, not how well they interview.

Prompt 2: Build an Interview Plan for Multiple Rounds

I'm hiring for a [Job Title] and will have [2/3/4] interview rounds. Here's the role:

[Paste job description or summarize]

Design an interview plan that covers everything without redundancy:

  • Round 1: [Phone screen / Recruiter screen] — what to assess, suggested questions, time allocation
  • Round 2: [Hiring manager interview] — what to assess, suggested questions, time allocation
  • Round 3: [Team/panel interview] — what to assess, who should ask what, time allocation
  • [Round 4 if applicable]: [Skills assessment / Final round]

Make sure each round has a distinct purpose. Tell me who should be in each round and what they should evaluate.

Prompt 3: Evaluate a Candidate's Answers

I just interviewed a candidate for [Job Title]. Here are the questions I asked and how they responded. Help me evaluate their answers:

Question 1: [Your question]
Their answer: [What they said, as best you remember]

Question 2: [Your question]
Their answer: [What they said]

[Continue for all questions]

For each answer:

  1. Rate it 1-5 (using the scorecard scale: 1 = doesn't meet, 3 = meets, 5 = exceptional)
  2. Explain what was strong about the answer
  3. Flag anything concerning
  4. Suggest a follow-up question I should have asked

Then give me an overall assessment: should I advance this candidate?

Prompt 4: Prepare for Hard Conversations During Interviews

I'm about to interview someone for [Job Title] and I need to address some potentially awkward topics:

[Describe what you need to discuss — e.g., "The role pays less than their current salary" / "There's a gap in their resume I want to understand" / "They were let go from their last position" / "The role requires relocation and they seem hesitant"]

Help me:

  1. Frame the topic professionally without being evasive
  2. Give me the exact wording to open the conversation
  3. Suggest follow-up questions based on likely responses
  4. Tell me what would be a reassuring answer vs. a concerning one

Prompt 5: Debrief and Compare Candidates

I've completed interviews for [Job Title]. Help me make a decision. Here's a summary of each candidate:

Candidate A: [Name]

  • Background: [Brief summary]
  • Strengths: [What stood out]
  • Concerns: [What worried you]
  • Compensation ask: [$X]
  • Interviewer recommendations: [Strong yes / Yes / Lean yes / etc.]

Candidate B: [Name]
[Same format]

Candidate C: [Name]
[Same format]

The role's top priorities are: [list the 2-3 most important things for success in this role]

Help me:

  1. Rank the candidates against those priorities
  2. Identify which concerns are dealbreakers vs. manageable
  3. Flag anything I might be overlooking
  4. Make a recommendation with your reasoning

Prompt 6: Practice Interviewing (Mock Interview)

I'm hiring for the first time and want to practice being the interviewer. Pretend you're a candidate for this role:

[Paste job description]

Play a realistic candidate — not a perfect one. Give me a mix of strong answers, okay answers, and at least one answer that should be a yellow or red flag. Answer in a conversational tone, not bullet points.

After I've asked 8-10 questions, break character and give me feedback on:

  • How well I probed for specifics
  • Whether I missed any red flags
  • Questions I should have asked but didn't
  • How the overall flow felt

Prompt 7: Create a Skills Assessment

I need a take-home or on-site skills assessment for a [Job Title] candidate. The role's key skills are:

[List 3-5 core skills — e.g., "data analysis in Excel, writing client-facing reports, project prioritization"]

Design a practical assessment that:

  • Takes no more than [30 minutes / 1 hour / 2 hours] to complete
  • Tests real skills they'd use on the job, not trick questions
  • Has a clear scoring rubric so multiple evaluators assess consistently
  • Respects the candidate's time (don't ask for free work that benefits your company)

Include the assessment itself, the instructions you'd send to the candidate, and the evaluation rubric for your team.
