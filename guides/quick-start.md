# Quick-Start Guide

You just bought the Hiring Toolkit. Here's how to get value from it today.

---

## First Hire? Start Here

If you've never hired before, do these three things:

1. **Write your job description** — Open `templates/01-job-description.md`, pick the template closest to your role, and fill it in. Takes 20-30 minutes.

2. **Prep your interview** — Open `templates/02-interview-questions.md` and pick 8-12 questions. Write them down in order so you're not scrambling during the conversation.

3. **Print a scorecard** — Open `templates/03-interview-scorecard.md` and fill in the role-specific competencies before your first interview. Print one copy per candidate.

That's enough to run a professional hiring process. Use the rest of the kit as you need it.

---

## Experienced Hirer? Skip Ahead

If you've hired before but want to tighten your process:

- **Standardize evaluation** — The scorecard (`03`) and comparison matrix (`04`) eliminate the "who did we like more?" debate. Use them with your team.
- **Speed up writing** — The AI prompts (`prompts/job-description-generator.md`) can draft a job description in 2 minutes. Edit it instead of writing from scratch.
- **Fix your rejections** — Most companies ghost candidates. The rejection templates (`06`) take 60 seconds to customize and send. Your employer brand will thank you.

## Using the AI Prompts

The prompt packs work with any AI tool — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or whatever you prefer.

**How to use them:**
1. Open the prompt file
2. Copy the prompt you need
3. Replace the bracketed `[text]` with your details
4. Paste into your AI tool
5. Edit the output — AI gives you 80%, your judgment finishes the job

**Best prompts to start with:**
- `job-description-generator.md → Prompt 5` (Brain Dump) — just talk about what you need and let AI structure it
- `interview-coach.md → Prompt 1` (Custom Questions) — generates role-specific interview questions with scoring guidance
- `hiring-operations.md → Prompt 4` (Build a Process) — gives you a complete hiring workflow if you don't have one

## Importing into Your Tools

### Google Docs / Word
Copy the template content and paste it into a new document. The markdown formatting translates cleanly into headers, bullets, and tables.

### Notion
1. Create a new page
2. Use `/markdown` or paste directly — Notion converts markdown automatically
3. For the scorecard and comparison matrix, consider converting tables to Notion databases for easier filtering and sorting

### Airtable / Spreadsheets
The comparison matrix (`04`) works best as a spreadsheet. Create columns for each candidate and rows for each competency. Use the 1-5 scoring scale and add formulas for averages.

### ATS (Applicant Tracking System)
If you use an ATS (Greenhouse, Lever, BambooHR, etc.):
- Import the scorecard fields as custom interview evaluation criteria
- Use the interview question bank to populate structured interview kits
- Set up rejection email templates using the templates in `06`

## Hiring Timeline: What a Typical Process Looks Like

| Day | Activity |
|-----|----------|
| **Day 1** | Write and post job description |
| **Days 2-14** | Collect applications, screen resumes |
| **Days 7-14** | Phone screens (20-30 min each) |
| **Days 14-21** | Full interviews (45-60 min each) |
| **Day 21-23** | Complete scorecards, run comparison matrix, team debrief |
| **Day 23-25** | Extend offer |
| **Day 25-30** | Negotiate if needed, get signed offer letter |
| **Day 30+** | Start onboarding |

This is a 30-day process for a single role. Adjust based on your urgency and candidate volume.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Writing requirements you don't actually need.** Every extra "must-have" filters out candidates who would've been great. Be honest about what's truly required.

2. **Interviewing without a plan.** Winging it means you're testing chemistry, not capability. Pick your questions in advance and ask every candidate the same ones.

3. **Waiting too long to decide.** Good candidates have options. If your process takes 6 weeks, you'll lose them to a company that moves in 3.

4. **Skipping the scorecard.** "We'll just compare notes" leads to whoever spoke last in the debrief winning the argument. Score independently, then discuss.

5. **Ghosting rejected candidates.** It takes 2 minutes to send a rejection email. The candidate spent hours on your process. Respect that.
