Quick-Start Guide

You just bought the Hiring Toolkit. Here's how to get value from it today.

First Hire? Start Here

If you've never hired before, do these three things:

  1. Write your job description — Open 01-job-description.html, pick the template closest to your role, and fill it in. Takes 20-30 minutes.
  2. Prep your interview — Open 02-interview-questions.html and pick 8-12 questions. Write them down in order so you're not scrambling during the conversation.
  3. Print a scorecard — Open 03-interview-scorecard.html and fill in the role-specific competencies before your first interview. Print one copy per candidate.

That's enough to run a professional hiring process. Use the rest of the kit as you need it.

Experienced Hirer? Skip Ahead

If you've hired before but want to tighten your process:

  • Standardize evaluation — The scorecard (03) and comparison matrix (04) eliminate the "who did we like more?" debate. Use them with your team.
  • Speed up writing — The AI prompts (job-description-generator.html) can draft a job description in 2 minutes. Edit it instead of writing from scratch.
  • Fix your rejections — Most companies ghost candidates. The rejection templates (06) take 60 seconds to customize and send. Your employer brand will thank you.

Using the AI Prompts

The prompt packs work with any AI tool — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or whatever you prefer.

How to use them:

  1. Open the prompt file
  2. Copy the prompt you need
  3. Replace the bracketed [text] with your details
  4. Paste into your AI tool
  5. Edit the output — AI gives you 80%, your judgment finishes the job

Best prompts to start with:

  • Job Description Generator — Prompt 5 (Brain Dump) — just talk about what you need and let AI structure it
  • Interview Coach — Prompt 1 (Custom Questions) — generates role-specific interview questions with scoring guidance
  • Hiring Operations — Prompt 4 (Build a Process) — gives you a complete hiring workflow if you don't have one

Using and Sharing Your Templates

Every template works directly in your browser — fill in fields, check boxes, and your work saves automatically. No apps or accounts needed.

  • Share the HTML file directly — send it to a colleague and they can open it in any browser, online or offline
  • Export as PDF — click "Save / Print PDF" in the toolbar for a clean printable version
  • Each person gets their own saved state — entries are stored locally in the browser, so multiple people can use the same template independently

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  1. Writing requirements you don't actually need. Every extra "must-have" filters out candidates who would've been great. Be honest about what's truly required.
  2. Interviewing without a plan. Winging it means you're testing chemistry, not capability. Pick your questions in advance and ask every candidate the same ones.
  3. Waiting too long to decide. Good candidates have options. If your process takes 6 weeks, you'll lose them to a company that moves in 3.
  4. Skipping the scorecard. "We'll just compare notes" leads to whoever spoke last in the debrief winning the argument. Score independently, then discuss.
  5. Ghosting rejected candidates. It takes 2 minutes to send a rejection email. The candidate spent hours on your process. Respect that.
